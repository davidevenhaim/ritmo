Nocturne Fit
A social gym log. Progress is the front door: streak, steps, goals, the week’s shape, then the plan. Sessions get built on a timeline; studios and coaches sell in.
Three tabs
Me — streak, stats, goals, week, plan, feed. Start — raised in the middle, opens the routine picker. Community — studios, coaches and the AI coach. The exercise library and the coach dashboard sit under Me.
Exercise media is a placeholder — the grey tiles are where the looping GIFs sit. I can’t generate imagery; drop real files in and they slot straight into the same frames.
